# What EventMate does not know yet

Vendor records are sample data, not live quotes. Treat every price as an estimate until the vendor confirms it in writing.

The budget reality check is a lower bound built from the cheapest eligible vendor for each service. It is not a quote.

Cultural and ritual guidance is not provided, because no reviewer from those communities has checked it. Dietary checklists are drafts and are marked unreviewed.

Times are local wall-clock times. Time zones, vendor working hours and multi-day events are not supported yet.

Amounts are in Indian rupees only.
